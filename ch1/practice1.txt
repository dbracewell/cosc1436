The Loan Officer's Rules:

+ Automatic Denial: If the applicant's credit score is below 620, they are automatically "DENIED".
+ Automatic Approval: If the credit score is 620 or higher and their income is at least $60,000, they are "APPROVED".
+ Manual Review: If they have a good credit score (620+) but make less than $60,000, they don't automatically fail, but a human needs to look at the file. The status should be "MANUAL REVIEW".
